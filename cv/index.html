Professional Summary

Robotics researcher building robust perception and robust state estimation for robots that must keep working when sensing degrades — underwater scattering and marine snow, sparse or biased depth, and environments that change over time. First author of three journal papers (IEEE Sensors Journal ×2, MDPI Sensors), co-author of papers in IJRR, IEEE T-FR, RA-L, and ICRA, and co-inventor of two registered Korean patents.
